## 7. Patterns (what wins / what gets built)

1. **Winner archetype: shopping/commerce voice agent.** ShopperMan = paste-link → AI analysis + alternatives + personal shopping assistant. Matches Omnidimension's voice/commerce sweet spot and both editions' reseller-deal-hunter entries (Nikunj, Shivam) — judges reward *transactional* voice AI over generic chatbots.
2. **Health vertical dominates volume:** Prenatal Nurture, Sehat AI, EchoMind, Crisis Haven = 4 of 7 known 2.0 builds. None confirmed placed — high participation, no evidence it wins.
3. **Solo + surprise + 6–10h ⇒ scope discipline wins.** Only finished, demoable MVPs surface; the one documented ambitious agentic build (Cofoundr.ai) failed to finish. Winner's own framing: single-link, single-flow tool.
4. **No official results infra.** Zero winner pages across geekroom.co.in, Geek Space blog, Luma, Devpost, LinkedIn company page — the ONLY winner record is a self-reported LinkedIn post. Expect this gap for 1.0 entirely.
5. **Community loves back-to-back narratives.** Top engagement pattern: winner's repeat-victory post outperforms all participant posts; comment tone sets cultural norm (admiration + memes, no salt).
