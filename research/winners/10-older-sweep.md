## 6. Posting-pattern notes (LinkedIn behaviour for older Geek Room editions)

1. **Who posts: winners/finalists ≫ org ≫ sponsor.**
   - Old-edition winner proof lives almost entirely on *participant* profiles (Honors entries, celebratory posts tagging teammates + Geek Room + venue sponsor), not on Geek Room's company page.
   - Org pages (Geek Room / Code Cubicle / chapters) post *registrations, shortlists, mentorship* — e.g. "35 teams selected for INFRASHIFT", "3,700 builders for CC 6.0" — but no retroactive CC 1.0/2.0/3.0/CK 1.0 winner tables were retrievable.
   - Sponsor (Mastercard AI Garage) winner posts: NOT FOUND in sweep.
2. **Format.**
   - Winner posts: text + team photo / stage photo + teammate tags + hashtag stack (#ArtificialIntelligence #GenerativeAI #GeekRoom + sponsor tags e.g. `💼 Code Cubicle 💻 Mastercard AI Garage 🔧 JetBrains`); often a "key takeaways" listicle (Anurag Mandloi pattern); repo/carousel links uncommon — only GitHub repos (Shivan5h INSIGHTFLOW) and cert PDFs (Malaai) found off-LinkedIn.
   - Honours-only records (Somya Tripathi, Gauri Madaan): no dedicated post body in scrape — just award entries; harder to harvest without profile expansion.
   - No evidence of video/carousel winner reveals for CC 1–3 on LinkedIn; Instagram winner carousels also NOT FOUND (only promo reels/posts).
3. **Timing.**
   - Winner posts land within days of finale (Anurag ~Sep 2024 for 21 Sep finale; organising posts May 2024 for 19 May finale). No "late" winner dumps observed — if it wasn't posted in-event-week, it likely never was.
   - Devfolio project pages appear JS-rendered and empty to scrapers (`Projects (0)`), so contemporaneous LinkedIn/GitHub posts are the only surviving record.
4. **Prize mention rate: low.** Posts celebrate selection/win + symposium/pitch opportunity; cash figures (20K CC3 / 40K CC2 / 10K CC1 / 25K CK1) appear only on Devfolio/event pages, essentially never in winner posts scraped.
5. **Implication for sourcing:** to complete winner tables, next step needs (a) browser-rendered Devfolio leaderboard scrape (`/leaderboard`, `/projects` with JS), (b) Instagram `@geek__room` / `@code_cubicle_hack` Sep 2024 + May 2024 + Aug 2024 winner carousels, (c) LinkedIn authenticated search for "Code Cubicle 3.0" / "Code Kshetra" post text (firecrawl Alexandria/enrichment unavailable in this runtime), (d) Unstop pages for CC 5.0-style successors that sometimes backfill past winners.
