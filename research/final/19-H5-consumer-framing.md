# H5 — Consumer-Framed Onboarding Beats Infra Framing

**Claim:** Framing onboarding as a consumer outcome ("Day-1 ready like unboxing your phone") beats infra/platform framing ("unified provisioning orchestration") on judge/user comprehension and signup intent.

**Null (H0):** Framing makes no difference; comprehension scores and signup-intent rates differ by <10% between arms.

**Falsification criterion (specific observation that kills H5):** In a 2-arm message test (n≥30, same product, only headline + 2-sentence subcopy differ), if consumer arm does NOT beat infra arm by ≥20% on comprehension (correct "what does it do" pick) AND ≥15% on signup intent (CTA click / top-2 Likert), H5 is FALSIFIED.

**Alternative (if falsified):** Lead with infra/platform framing for technical judges; keep consumer line as secondary.

**Priority:** P1 — decides pitch opener, landing copy, and Track 1 story.

**Verification method:**
1. Arm A (consumer): "First day feels like unboxing — laptop, logins, payroll just work." Arm B (infra): "Cross-system HR-IT orchestration with join resolution."
2. Randomized survey/poster test (n≥30 devs/students); measure: 1-question comprehension + 5-pt signup intent + CTA click.
3. Segment by technical vs non-technical to check judge-fit interaction.

**Expected outcome:** Consumer arm +20–30% comprehension, +15–25% intent; qualitative comments mention "I get it instantly." Action on confirm: open pitch + README with consumer frame, relegate architecture to "how it works" section.
