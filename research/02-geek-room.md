## 5. What judges/community likely reward [inference — labelled]

1. **Working product over idea:** every listing stresses "real-world AI-powered solutions", "working prototype", "production-ready" (Luma: https://luma.com/k1fova8c ; HackBLR: https://www.geekroom.co.in/event/hackblr1 ; Global AI: https://www.geekroom.co.in/event/global-ai-hackathon).
2. **Visible use of the sponsor stack (Synapse CLI):** precedent — Pears required P2P integration (https://www.geekroom.co.in/event/pears); Global AI requires Bifrost/Tavily + public GitHub repo + PPT (https://aihackathon.app/e/geekroom-global-ai-2026). Expect equivalent weight on Synapse/MCP usage; start at https://2ndbrainlabs.ai and docs https://synapse.2ndbrainlabs.ai/docs.
3. **Build-in-public energy:** homepage mantra "hack, ship, and meet"; "Building in public is the way forward" (https://www.geekroom.co.in/). LinkedIn/Instagram reels culture around announcements (e.g. https://www.linkedin.com/posts/geekroom-community_vibeathon-hackathon-geekroom-activity-7524056550320033792-Oa8N ; https://www.instagram.com/p/Dfzgh54STB4/).
4. **Professional polish:** this edition is professionals-only (https://luma.com/k1fova8c) — demos, problem fit, and industry networking matter more than in student editions.
5. **Voice/agent tracks have history:** Vapi, Omnidimension, MiniMax recur (https://www.geekroom.co.in/partners) — agent/voice UX polish likely scores well.
